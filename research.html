Euclid’s Elements in India

A poster presentation about Euclid's Elements in India. MTA Conference APU - 2024.

Elements is a pioneering mathematical treatise on geometry and number theory authored by Euclid. The book reached India in the 18th and 19th centuries and has been translated into several Indian languages. These translations remain inaccessible to the students and people interested in geometry.
