I am Manikandan Sambasivam, currently a Project Scientific Officer in the Vigyan Pratibha program at the Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc), Chennai. My work focuses on improving the teaching and learning of mathematics and science through workshops for teachers, outreach programmes for school and college students, and general public. I am interested in translating scientific and mathematical works into Tamil. I also write and translate fiction and non-fiction.
